move a todo

who       Someone re-prioritising by hand: the thing they will do first should be
          at the top of its section, not buried third.

given     A list with at least two todos in one section.

expect    The two lines exchange places. Both texts survive byte for byte, every
          other line stays where it was, and nothing is created or lost.

why       Order is information in a todo list; that is what a list is for. Moving a
          todo must be a swap of lines, not a delete-and-add: a delete-and-add
          loses the line's identity, and would drop any metadata anchored to it.

behaviour A heading is a wall, because a todo belongs to the section it is written
          under: a move stops there rather than filing it under the next heading.
          Prose and blank lines are not walls — the todo steps over them and they
          stay where they are.
