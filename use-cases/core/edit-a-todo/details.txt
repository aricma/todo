edit a todo

who       Someone whose todo was too vague to act on. "pay rent" becomes "pay
          rent and the water bill" — the same task, said properly.

given     A list with a todo whose text needs changing.

expect    The same line, in the same place, with the same box and priority, and a
          different text. The tags and assignees in the new text are the ones the
          new text has; nothing else in the file moves.

why       Editing the text is not re-creating the task. A person who fixes a typo
          must not lose the tick, and must not lose the priority they thought
          about.

behaviour The text is edited where the todo sits, so what is being changed is
          visible against its neighbours. Committing writes that one line;
          cancelling leaves it untouched.
